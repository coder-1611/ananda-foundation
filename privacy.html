Your Privacy

Privacy Policy

The short version: this website collects no personal information at all.

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Who we are

Ananda Foundation is a nonprofit organization working in tribal and rural Bihar, India. This website (www.anandafoundation.net) is an informational site about our programs, impact, and campaigns.

What we collect

Nothing. This is a static informational website:

  • There are no user accounts and nothing to sign up for.
  • There are no contact forms — the site stores nothing you type, because there is nowhere to type anything.
  • We set no cookies and use no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts of any kind.
  • We collect no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

If you email us

If you choose to contact us at anandafoundation1@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you, and we never sell or share it.

Hosting

Like nearly every website, our hosting provider may keep standard, short-lived server logs (such as IP addresses and requested pages) for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify visitors.

Links to other sites

The site links to a small number of external pages, such as our LinkedIn profile. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them.

Changes & contact

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions are always welcome at anandafoundation1@gmail.com.

Empowering communities, enriching lives. Working in tribal and rural Bihar since 2019.
